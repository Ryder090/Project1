### FAQ
**1. How does the audit tool work?**
You simply select the AI tools your team uses and input your current headcount/spend. Our engine analyzes the stack for overlapping capabilities, incorrect tier usage, and un-utilized credits, then spits out a report.

**2. Do I need to connect my bank account or SSO?**
No. We don't ask for OAuth, Plaid integrations, or any read-access to your company data. It's a completely manual, privacy-first calculator. 

**3. What happens if I find savings?**
If your audit finds that you are overpaying for retail subscriptions (like ChatGPT Enterprise or Claude Team), you'll have the option to book a consultation with Credex to migrate your billing and secure discounted enterprise credits.

**4. How does Credex offer discounted credits?**
Credex brokers unused AI infrastructure credits from startups that have over-forecasted or pivoted, allowing you to buy the exact same tier of service at a 20-30% discount.

**5. Is the audit actually free?**
Yes. The audit is 100% free to use. We only make money if you choose to buy discounted credits through Credex.
