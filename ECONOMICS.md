# Unit Economics

Here's the back-of-the-napkin math for how this free tool actually drives revenue for Credex. 

### The Value of a Converted Lead
Let's assume the average target company using this tool has 30 employees.
- Average AI spend per employee (across all tools) = ~$50/mo.
- Total company AI spend = $1,500/mo ($18,000/year).
- If Credex can provide credits at a 20% discount, they save the company $3,600/year.
- Assuming Credex takes a 30% margin on the discounted credits they broker, the gross profit to Credex per converted company is **~$1,080/year**.

Therefore, a single converted B2B lead is worth roughly **$1,000 in ARR** to Credex.

### CAC by Channel
If we look at the GTM plan, we are entirely focused on $0 budget, high-leverage channels for the first 30 days:
1. **Direct X/Twitter Outreach (Targeting fractional CFOs):**
   - Time cost: 10 hours/week.
   - CAC: ~$0 (pure sweat equity).
2. **Product Hunt Launch:**
   - Graphic/video prep: $200 (Fiverr/Upwork).
   - Expected leads: ~300 audits -> 10 converted customers.
   - CAC: **$20**.
3. **HN / Reddit (r/SaaS, r/startups):**
   - Time cost: 2 hours.
   - Expected leads: ~50 audits -> 2 converted customers.
   - CAC: **$0**.

Blended MVP CAC is essentially zero, barring the founder's time.

### The Conversion Funnel
To make this profitable and worth the engineering effort, we need to look at the funnel from audit to closed deal. 
Here are realistic B2B SaaS conversion rates for a free diagnostic tool:
- **Audit Completed (100% of top-of-funnel)**
- **Email Captured for Report:** 40% (Only 4/10 will actually give their email after seeing the teaser savings).
- **Consultation Booked:** 5% of captured emails. (2% of total).
- **Credit Purchase (Closed Deal):** 40% of consultations. (0.8% of total).

**Math:** For every 1,000 people who run an audit, 8 will buy credits.
At an LTV of $1,000/deal, 1,000 audits = **$8,000 in gross profit**.

### Path to $1M ARR
To hit $1,000,000 in ARR via this tool in 18 months, what has to be true?
At $1k ARR per customer, we need 1,000 closed deals.
If our end-to-end conversion rate is 0.8%, we need **125,000 audits completed** in 18 months.
That's ~7,000 audits a month.

To achieve that, the tool *must* have a viral loop. Direct outreach won't scale to 7k audits/mo. The "Shareable Report" feature (with clean OpenGraph previews) is the most critical piece. If every founder who saves $5,000 shares their unique `/report/xyz` link in their investor update or private founder Slack groups, the K-factor could push this to 10k+ audits a month organically.
